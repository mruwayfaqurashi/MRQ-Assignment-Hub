A Journey Through Technological History

The Industrial Revolution (1760 - 1840)

The Industrial Revolution marked the first major turning point in technological history. Machines powered by steam replaced manual labor in factories, and inventions such as the steam engine, spinning machines, and the telegraph transformed how goods were produced and information was shared across long distances.

The Computer Age (1940 - 1990)

The 20th century brought the birth of the computer. What started as room-sized machines used for military calculations slowly evolved into personal computers that ordinary people could use at home and work. This era laid the foundation for everything digital that followed.

Key Milestones of the Computer Age

  1. 1946 - ENIAC, one of the first electronic general-purpose computers, is built.
  2. 1971 - The first microprocessor, the Intel 4004, is released.
  3. 1976 - Apple is founded, popularizing personal computers.
  4. 1981 - IBM launches the first widely adopted personal computer (IBM PC).
  5. 1985 - Microsoft releases Windows 1.0.

The Internet Revolution (1990 - 2010)

The creation of the World Wide Web by Tim Berners-Lee in 1989 changed everything. Within a decade, the internet connected millions of people worldwide, giving rise to email, search engines, e-commerce, and eventually social media. Mobile phones also became smaller, smarter, and more powerful during this period, setting the stage for the smartphone era.

Notable Events

  • 1991 - The World Wide Web becomes publicly available.
  • 1998 - Google is founded.
  • 2004 - Facebook launches, beginning the social media era.
  • 2007 - Apple releases the first iPhone, launching the smartphone revolution.
